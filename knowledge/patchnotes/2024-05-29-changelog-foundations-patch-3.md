# Changelog: Foundations Patch #3

_date: 2024-05-29 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5742731639325939309_

### Foundations Patch 3 - Version 25.030.1 - May 29, 2024

### Goldmane Pegasus Added

To celebrate the successful launch of Albion Europe, we have added the brand-new **Goldmane Pegasus** skin to all accounts with Albion Europe Silver Founder Packs or higher! In addition, Albion Europe Crystal Founders receive the **Armored Goldmane Pegasus**. These skins are immediately available via the Appearance menu, and are non-tradable. Thank you to everyone for your support!

### Changes

- Time required to interact with Siege Banners increased from 0.5s to 1.5s

### Combat Balance Changes

**Axes**

- Bloody Reap (Infernal Scythe)
  - Damage when target below 40% health: 240 → 220
  - Damage when target above 40% health: 126 → 120

**Bows**

- Demon Arrow (Wailing Bow)
  - Cooldown: 30s → 25s

**Hammers**

- Dreadladen Fighting (all Hammers)
  - CC Strength Increase: 50% → 35%
  - Improved the visual effect

- Groundbreaker (Polehammer)
  - Improved visual feedback to better communicate the actual area speed
  - Stun Duration: 2.5s → 2.7s

**Spears**

- Fling (Glaive)
  - Base Damage: 50.90 → 56
  - 1 Spirit Spear Charge: 81.43 → 90
  - 2 Spirit Spear Charges: 122.15 → 134
  - 3 Spirit Spear Charges: 173.04 → 190

**Swords**

- Parry Strike (all Swords)
  - Reflect Damage: 100% → 125%

- Charge (Claymore)
  - Charge now deals damage to all enemies it passes through
  - Enemies passed through receive 40% of the damage that the target receives

**Maces**

- Defensive Slam (all Maces)
  - Duration: 3s → 4s

- Dreadladen Fighting (all Maces)
  - CC Strength Increase: 50% → 35%
  - Improved the visual effect

- Sacred Ground (all Maces)
  - Area Duration: 4s → 3s

- Blessed Aurora (Oathkeepers)
  - Increased max targets: 5 → 10
  - Healing range: 6m → 8m

**Quarterstaffs**
- Dreadladen Fighting (all Quarterstaffs)
  - Improved the visual effect

**War Gloves**
- Rushdown - Passive (War Gloves)
  - Movement Speed: 20% → 30%

- Shockwave (all War Gloves)
  - 1st Cast Cooldown: 4s → 3.5s
  - 2nd Cast Cooldown: 6s → 5s

- Counter (all War Gloves)
  - Added new combo attack:
    - Follow Through: For 4s, dash to the first target marked
    - Dash Range: 20m → 32m
    - Cooldown: 15s

**Helmets**

- Iceblock (Cleric Cowl)
  - Can no longer be canceled by the caster
  - Duration: 4.75s → 4s

**Armors**
- Life Steal Aura (Hellion Jacket)
- Damage when health above 80%: 20 → 16
- Damage when health above 60%: 25 → 20
- Damage when health above 40%: 30 → 24
- Damage when health above 20%: 35 → 28
- Damage when health below 20%: 40 → 32
**Miscellaneous**

- Dismounted mount range: 12m → 15m

### Fixes

- Spell fixes:
  - Groundbreaker (Polehammer): Fixed issue where tooltip displayed the wrong cast range
  - Starfall (Astral Staff): Fixed issue where tooltip displayed the wrong energy cost
  - Gravitas (Morning Star): Fixed issue where tooltip displayed range incorrectly

- UI fixes:
  - Fixed issue where, in Streamer Mode, email addresses would be visible on login verification page
  - Fixed issue where opening the map of a neighboring region with Fortifications would show the wrong upgrades
  - Fixed issue where Herb Garden products were not correctly sorted by tier

- Fixed issue where Shrine of Invisibility buff could cause mount to disappear in overcrowded regions
- Fixed issue where players could get stuck in a trade state after disconnecting mid-trade
- Fixed rare issue in duo mists where players in a party could briefly become hostile after Mists portal closed
- Fixed rare issue in Yellow Zone Mists where picking up Wounded Wisps twice while in combat could cause PvP-flagging to persist after leaving the Mists
