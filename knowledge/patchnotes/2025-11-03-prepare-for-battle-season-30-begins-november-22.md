# Prepare for Battle: Season 30 Begins November 22

_date: 2025-11-03 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1815034432999892_

**Season 30** begins **November 22**, just ahead of the [**Realm Divided**](https://albiononline.com/update/realm-divided) update. With new challenges on the horizon and shifting borders soon to reshape Albion, guilds are already preparing for what is set to be one interesting season. It’s time to prove your worth and write the next page in Albion’s ongoing saga!

Start times by region are as follows:

- **Albion Asia: November 22**, **00:00 UTC**
- **Albion Americas** and **Albion Europe**:** November 22**,** 11:00 UTC**
Those who rise to the **Conqueror’s Challenge** can claim loot-filled **Conqueror’s Chests**, unlock exclusive **Avatar Sets**, and enjoy **Fame buffs** to speed up their journey.

This season, there’s something special for collectors: **Avatar Sets** will feature fewer new avatars, but those will be replaced with chances to unlock avatars from **past seasons**. By the end of Season 30, you’ll have the opportunity to complete your collection with some of Albion’s most beloved looks!

### Season Schedule Highlights

- **November 22**: Season start and Invasion Day
- **December 20**: Midseason Invasion Day, double scoring begins
- **January 17**: Final Invasion Day
- **January 19**: Season ends
For the full season schedule, see this [**forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/218487-Season-30-Schedules/).

### New to Guild Seasons? Here's What You Need to Know!

**Guild Seasons** are a key part of Albion's competitive landscape, where guilds battle over two months to claim territories, earn Season Points, rise up the rankings and earn coveted rewards. Participating in a Guild Season allows you to contribute to your guild's success through various activities. Even if you’re not focused on PvP, you can still help your guild gather **Season Points** and unlock valuable rewards!
