# Dev Talk: Dragon Sanctuary & New Rewards

_date: 2026-08-28 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842212951302746_

It’s just a few days now until **Dragonfire** launches on **August 31**, and things are heating up in Albion. In our third and final Dev Talk previewing the update, Co-Creative Director **Michael Schwahn **and Combat Balance Designer **Nick Yaloz **take a look at the **Fire Dragon **itself, the new **Dragon Sanctuary **dungeon, and the rewards you can earn by taking on these immense challenges.

### The Dragon Sanctuary

The Dragon Sanctuary is a brand-new, 20-player raid dungeon filled with high-level PvE encounters.

To enter the Dragon Sanctuary, you first need a **Waystone**.

- These are rare rewards from the **Ancient Lands**
- They are tradable, so can also be acquired from the **Marketplace**
Using a Waystone in the **open world **will reveal an entrance to the Dragon Sanctuary. Only you and your party members can use this entrance.

- Because there is no PvP inside, groups should bring their **best PvE equipment**
- Mobs are **immune to forced movement**, so skill and strategy are needed
- The boss – **Maeve, the Unfettered Oracle** – is a powerful opponent who hurls boulders across the battlefield
Should you make your way past Maeve, an even tougher opponent awaits…

### Azaryon, the Fire Dragon

**Azaryon, the Fire Dragon** is the game’s greatest PvE challenge, and marks a step forward for PvE in Albion.

- An opponent of this **scale** has never been seen before
- **New movement and combat mechanics** were created for Azaryon
  - He reacts dynamically to your group’s **movement**, **positioning**, and **threat level**
  - This makes encounters **unpredictable** in new ways

- The fight evolves in **phases**, each with its own pacing, mechanics, and challenges
  - As the battle evolves, you must adapt your **strategy** and **coordination**

****

Defeating Azaryon gives every party member a **Lingering Dragon Spirit**, which lasts one week and makes it significantly harder to complete the Dragon Sanctuary. However, there are highly ambitious **Albion Journal** missions for those who can achieve repeat victories.

### Open-World Attacks

Dragons can not just be encountered in the Dragon Sanctuary, but also in the **open world**. On the **Royal Continent**:

- Dragon attacks are spontaneous events with **very little forewarning**
- Once a certain amount of damage has been dealt, the Dragon will **retreat**
- Retreating Dragons leave behind a **treasure chest**
  - **Every player** who participates in the encounter can earn loot from this chest
  - Even **solo players **should fight a Dragon attack when the opportunity arises

In the **Outlands**, meanwhile:

- Attacks are announced **hours in advance**
- This gives guilds time to **organize and prepare**
- Guilds compete both against the **Dragon** and against **each other** for its rewards
  - The victorious guild receives a large amount of **Season Points**
  - Individual players receive **personal reward chests**

### New Rewards

Dragonfire brings a number of valuable **new rewards **to players who conquer its challenges.

**Dragonslayer Armor**

This is a new **leather armor** set with unique new abilities.

- **Dragonslayer Hood: **Lets you toss a flare which reveals enemies and slows their cooldowns
- **Dragonslayer Jacket: **Fly to a targeted location, forming a protective barrier where you land
- **Dragonslayer Shoes: **Offer a choice between a significant movement speed increase and a cooldown reduction
These items can be crafted from **Fire Dragon Scales **or from **artifacts **dropped by Dragons.

**Drake Eggs**

A Drake Egg is a very rare reward which can be raised into a **Firewing Drake**.

- To raise a Firewing Drake you must feed it **other mounts**
- Firewing Drakes are very strong in **every area**
- They have a **glide **ability, a speed boost which **cannot be interrupted by damage**
  - Only a **stun **can interrupt this

**New Food and Potions**

- Drake Eggs can also be used to make **Drake Biscuits**
  - These offer a **combat and gathering Fame buff **for **25 hours**.

- **Leyfin Fish **can be caught in the Ancient Lands
  - These can be used to make an Omelette which increases **healing output**

- Dragon Blood can be used to create a **Lifeward Potion**
  - This can **protect allies from death **for a short time

With all this coming on **August 31**, [**Dragonfire**](https://albiononline.com/update/dragonfire) promises to expand Albion’s offerings immeasurably. From the depths of the Dragon Sanctuary to the skies above the open world, threats and opportunities of a scale never before seen are about to arrive. Get ready to face the heat!
