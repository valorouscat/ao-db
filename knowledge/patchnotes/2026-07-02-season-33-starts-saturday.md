# Season 33 Starts Saturday

_date: 2026-07-02 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165567571_

**Season 33** begins this **Saturday, July 4**, and while guilds once again prepare to fight for control of the Outlands, this season brings a new opportunity.

The start of the Guild Season also marks the beginning of the [**Keeper Uprising**](https://albiononline.com/news/keeper-uprising-starts-soon), with Keeper forces across Albion becoming a valuable source of **Might **and **Season Point** progress. Combined with the usual battles for land, power, and prestige, it promises a Guild Season unlike any before it.

**Start times** by server:

- **Albion Asia**: July 4, 00:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas **and **Albion Europe**: July 4, 11:00 UTC
For full season changes and schedules, see this [**forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/223209-Season-33-Schedules/).
