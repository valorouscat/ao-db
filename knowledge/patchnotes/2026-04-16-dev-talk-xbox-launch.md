# Dev Talk: Xbox Launch

_date: 2026-04-16 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1830163047257715_

Albion Online is coming to **Xbox Series X|S** on **April 21**, marking the game’s first step into the console space. This launch brings the full Albion experience to Xbox, with complete cross-platform play, shared servers, and a redesigned controller interface built for precision and ease-of-use.

In this video, **Co-Creative Director Michael Schwahn** introduces Albion’s Xbox launch, detailing how the game has been adapted for consoles and how you can experience it.

### A True Cross-Platform MMO

The Xbox launch brings the full Albion experience to console:

- The** same servers** across Xbox, PC, and mobile
- The **same characters, items, and progression** everywhere
- The ability to **switch between platforms** at any time
Account access is easy for both existing and new players:

- **Existing Albion Online accounts** can be linked on Xbox to continue progress
- New players can **start instantly** on Xbox without creating an Albion Online account
  - These can be **converted into an Albion Online account** at any time to enable full cross-platform play

### Designed for Controller Play

Alongside the Xbox launch, controller support has been significantly improved, making the game easy to pick up while preserving the depth needed for high-level gameplay.

Key improvements include:

- Reworked **targeting and aiming **for faster, more reliable combat
- Improved **visual clarity** to track and switch targets more easily
- Redesigned **button layouts** for a more natural flow

These changes ensure that combat remains responsive and competitive, especially in large-scale fights.

### New UI and Navigation Systems

To support controller gameplay, several new interface systems have been designed specifically for console use:

- A **full-screen game menu** for quick navigation between key systems
- **Shoulder-button tabbing** to move through menus and UI screens
- A reworked** radial menu** for fast access to actions and communication
- **Improved navigation** across systems like the Destiny Board and Party Management
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/a26197d0b1b0f511fd2e997c8d1ccbc338784076.jpeg)

**Controller support **is fully integrated **across all platforms**, so using a controller on PC or mobile provides the same experience.

### Xbox Rewards

Players who link their Albion account with Xbox Game Pass can unlock **exclusive rewards**:

- **Elite Green Knight** vanity bundle
- 3 days **Premium**
- **Fame** boosts and Tomes of Insight
- **Victory emotes**

These rewards are available to both new and existing players with an active **Xbox Game Pass subscription**.

The Xbox launch represents more than a new platform. It is a key milestone in Albion’s evolution as a fully cross-platform MMORPG, opening the door to a wider audience while improving the experience for everyone. Start your adventure on Xbox consoles on **April 21**, and forge your legend across Albion’s shared world!
