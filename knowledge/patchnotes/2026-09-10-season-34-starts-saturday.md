# Season 34 Starts Saturday

_date: 2026-09-10 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1843481262691697_

[**Season 34**](https://albiononline.com/news/season-34-starts-soon) begins this **Saturday, September 12**, as guilds prepare to battle for control of the Outlands while facing new challenges unleashed by the **Dragonfire** update. And this season brings exciting opportunities to earn **Might** through some serious dragonslaying!

Guilds can explore the **Ancient Lands**, battling rivals and hunting powerful creatures, mobilize to repel **Dragon Attacks** in the open world, or unite for the ultimate raid challenge in the **Dragon Sanctuary**.

Start times:

- **Albion Asia**: September 12, 00:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas **and **Albion Europe**: September 12, 11:00 UTC
For full season changes and schedules, see this [**forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/224318-Season-34-Schedules/).
