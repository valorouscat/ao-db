# Changelog: Dragonfire Update

_date: 2026-08-31 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842212951309746_

> _This update introduces dragons to Albion, from open world attacks to the Dragon Sanctuary, where adventurers will face the most challenging raid battle to date against Azaryon, the Fire Dragon. Players can also explore the mysterious Ancient Lands and earn powerful new rewards, including brand-new Dragonslayer Armor, consumables, and the elite Firewing Drake mount. _

### Ancient Lands

Dragonfire unveils a previously undiscovered realm: the Ancient Lands. This domain comprises numerous floating islands suspended over Albion, linked by a network of Leygates. Upon arrival, adventurers will face Ashborn mobs, brand-new creatures, and rival parties. Activating Leygates allows deeper exploration into the territory, where even more significant challenges and rewards await those who venture furthest.

**Entering the Ancient Lands**

The Ancient Lands can be reached via portals in the open world.
- In Yellow Zones, portals take you to partial-loot, orange-zone Ancient Lands where only inventory is dropped on death; in Black Zones, portals take you to full-loot Ancient Lands
  - The Ancient Lands have a 1200 IP soft cap
  - This soft cap scales differently in full-loot and partial-loot Ancient Lands
    - Orange zone: 20%
    - Black zone: 35%

- Portals are displayed on the Minimap and Region Map, as well as the World Map within a certain range
- There are different portals available for different group sizes:
  - Small: 1-5 players
  - Medium: 5-7 players
  - Large: 15-20 players

- The number of players entering a portal determines which matchmaking pool you join:
  - Small:
    - Solo players face other solo players
    - Groups of 2-3 players are matched against other groups of 2-3
    - Groups of 4-5 players are matched against other groups of 4-5

  - Medium: Groups of 5-7 players are matched against other groups of 5-7
  - Large: Groups of 15-20 players are matched against other groups of 15-20

- Once a portal appears, it is initially locked, and can be entered after a set amount of time:
  - Small: 30 minutes
  - Medium: 60 minutes
  - Large: 180 minutes

- After opening, portals remain accessible for a duration of three minutes
- To enter a portal, every party member must declare themselves ready and channel for a short time
  - Finishing the channel grants players temporary invulnerability to damage
  - Once all players have channeled, the party leader decides exactly when the party enters

- Within the Ancient Lands, parties are anonymous to one another; you can only see the name of your fellow party members
  - Each group arrives at a separate spawn location

**Within the Ancient Lands**

When you arrive in the Ancient Lands, you’ll appear on one of the outer islands, and you’ll see two quests in your HUD Tracker:
- A local quest, tied to the island you are currently exploring
- A global quest, which culminates in unlocking the final encounter
Defeating the enemies on an island completes its local quest, rewarding you with a Leygate Keystone:
- Leygate Keystones are used to activate the Leygates that connect the various islands of the Ancient Lands
- These can be used on any Leygate in the Ancient Lands
- Each island has several Leygates which connect to different islands
- Keystones can be removed from an active Leygate, allowing you to carry them elsewhere and activate a different route
- Activated Leygates are displayed on the Region Map, with newly activated connections briefly highlighted
As you explore, you will encounter four Drakes on certain islands, marked on both the Minimap and Region Map. You must complete each of these islands’ quests to complete the global quest.
- Once the global quest has been completed, the central island unlocks after a short delay, granting access to the Ancient Lands' final boss: the Fire Drake
- Defeating the Fire Drake awards a large amount of loot and opens a portal back to the open-world region from which you entered
If you wish to leave before completing the encounter, any entry portal found around the outer islands can be used. These return you to your original open-world region.

**Spirit Essence**

Spirit Essence is a stackable buff that can only be acquired and used within the Ancient Lands.
- Each stack increases Fame earned and grants increased damage and defense against mobs
- Spirit Essence can be obtained in several ways:
  - Interacting with Dragon Statues, which grant Spirit Essence to you and your party
  - Defeating mobs, which have a chance to award Spirit Essence to your party
    - Certain enemies, such as Drakes, always grant Spirit Essence

  - Defeating players in PvP
    - If you are knocked down in PvP, 50% of your current Spirit Essence is transferred to the opposing group, while the remainder is lost

- Spirit Essence is lost when you leave the Ancient Lands

**Revive**

In both partial-loot and full-loot Ancient Lands, players who are knocked down in PvP enter a Bleedout state instead of dying immediately. While in Bleedout, players can be revived by allies before a timer expires.
- A bar displayed on-screen indicates the amount of time in which a revival is possible
- Moving away from your downed party member causes this timer to deplete more quickly
- If no allies remain to perform a revival, Bleedout ends in a short time
- Revived players return with a stacking 10% maximum Health debuff
  - This effect lasts only while inside the Ancient Lands

**Ancient Altars**

Ancient Altars can be found throughout the Ancient Lands, with a guaranteed Altar on the central island and additional Altars spawning on smaller islands adjacent to the spawn location.
- Ancient Bones, obtained from Drakes and sometimes chests, can be offered at Ancient Altars to earn Draconic Insight, Fame, and temporary Fame bonuses
  - Small sacrifices grant a 10% Fame bonus for one hour, while large sacrifices grant a 20% Fame bonus for two hours
  - Nearby allies also receive rewards when a ritual is completed

- See ‘New Rewards’ section below for more information on Draconic Insight and its associated rewards

### Dragon Sanctuary

The Dragon Sanctuary is Albion’s most challenging dedicated PvE encounter to date, built for raids of up to 20 players. Fight through a dangerous Ashborn stronghold, overcome powerful high-level bosses, and ultimately face Azaryon, the Fire Dragon.

**Access**
- To enter the Dragon Sanctuary, you need a Spirit Waystone
- Spirit Waystones can be obtained by:
  - Collecting Waystone Fragments in the Ancient Lands and combining them at the Artifact Foundry
  - Receiving a complete one as a drop from Fire Drakes or Dragons
  - Buying either Waystones or their fragments from other players or on the Marketplace, as both are tradable

- Spirit Waystones range from Tier 6 to Tier 8.4
  - These determine the tier of the Dragon Sanctuary they open

- Using a Spirit Waystone in an open-world region of the corresponding tier creates a portal to a Dragon Sanctuary
- Each Dragon Sanctuary can be entered by a single raid of up to 20 players

**The Dragon Sanctuary**

The Dragon Sanctuary contains new Ashborn enemies, powerful Drakes, deadly traps, and increasingly demanding PvE encounters. There is no Item Power cap, allowing groups to bring their strongest PvE gear and optimized compositions.

Guarding the path to the Dragon Sanctuary's heart is Maeve, the Unfettered Oracle. As the leader of the Ashborn, this formidable high-level encounter serves as the ultimate test for your party before the raid's final boss: Azaryon, the Fire Dragon.

### Azaryon, the Fire Dragon

Awaiting at the end of the Dragon Sanctuary is Azaryon, Albion's most challenging raid boss to date.

Designed for up to twenty players, the encounter demands coordination, execution, and teamwork across multiple phases featuring devastating attacks and unique mechanics. Players capable of defeating Azaryon can earn immense rewards, including a rare Drake Egg (see ‘New Rewards’ below).

If your party defeats Azaryon, every member will receive a Lingering Dragon Spirit, lasting for seven days. Each Lingering Dragon Spirit makes completing the Dragon Sanctuary significantly more difficult, making repeat attempts a considerable challenge.

### Dragon Attacks

Dragons also launch attacks across both the Royal Continent and the Outlands, creating large-scale, open-world encounters that any player can join.
- On the Royal Continent, attacks are announced only shortly beforehand to encourage spontaneous encounters
- In the Outlands, they are announced much earlier, allowing groups to prepare and contest the objective
As the dragon approaches, its roar echoes across the region and its shadow sweeps overhead before it descends in a fiery landing, scorching the surrounding area and challenging everyone nearby.

Reduce the dragon's health until it is forced to retreat. As it flees, it leaves behind a reward chest containing loot for every player who contributed to the fight, whether playing solo or as part of a group. In the Outlands, successfully repelling a Dragon Attack also awards Might and Favor, making these encounters valuable Guild Season objectives.

### New Rewards

Dragonfire introduces a host of new challenges and rewards, from powerful equipment and consumables to exclusive cosmetics.

**Draconic Insight**

The more you encounter Dragons and explore the Ancient Lands, the more you learn about them. Draconic Insight is a new form of Standing which can be increased through the following activities:
- Interacting with Dragon Statues
- Sacrificing Bones to an Ancient Altar
- Defeating Drakes and Dragons
Increasing Draconic Insight unlocks brand-new rewards, including the **Ancient Mystic** **Vanity Set**, **Avatar**, and **Avatar Ring**.

**Dragon Armor**

Fire Dragon Scales can be obtained by defeating Drakes and Dragons, and used to craft the new Dragonslayer Armor set. Each piece grants a unique ability:
- **Dragonslayer Jacket:** Creates a protective zone that grants immunity to enemies outside it while increasing your damage output and cooldown recovery
- **Dragonslayer Hood:** Launches a flare that reveals invisible enemies and slows their cooldown recovery
- **Dragonslayer Shoes:** Build up stacks of movement speed that can be consumed to instantly refund your weapon cooldowns

**Drake Mount**

Defeating Azaryon, the Fire Dragon, offers a rare chance to obtain a Fire Drake Egg. These can be raised in a Kennel into a Firewing Drake, but be warned: these creatures have quite the appetite, and you'll need to feed it other mounts before it's ready to ride.
- The Firewing Drake is immune to forced movement effects
- Rather than entering a traditional gallop, the Firewing Drake glides across Albion
  - While gliding, it is immune to roots and slows
  - Unlike a normal gallop, taking damage will not interrupt its glide
    - Only stuns can end the effect

- The Firewing Drake can also unleash a scorching breath of fire, dealing damage over time to enemies caught in its path

**New Consumables**

Drake Eggs can also serve a rather different purpose in the kitchen:
- **Drake Egg Biscuits:** Increases Combat and Gathering Fame for 25 hours
  - This bonus does not replace or remove combat food buffs, allowing it to be used alongside regular combat food

Drakes and Dragons can also drop Dragon Blood, a rare crafting material used to create a powerful new potion:
- **Lifeward Potion:** Protects nearby allies from a fatal blow, reviving them the first time they would be defeated
The Ancient Lands are also home to a previously unknown type of fish. These Leyfin fish can be used to cook a new dish:
- **Leyfin Omelettes:** Provides cast speed reduction, cooldown reduction, and a healing cast bonus

**New Albion Journal Missions**

Dragonfire brings new missions in the Creatures, Exploration, and PvE categories. These focus on hunting Drakes and Dragons, exploring the Ancient Lands, and raiding the Dragon Sanctuary.

**New Guild Might Levels**

Guild members can now earn Might and Season Points through two new Guild Might Levels, one focused on the Ancient Lands and one on Dragon encounters.

**Ancient Memories**

New Lore Fragments can be found throughout the Ancient Lands, uncovering the history of Dragons and their connection to Albion.
- Each Ancient Memory is permanently recorded in a new Lore category in the Albion Journal, where you'll also find hints to help locate those you have yet to discover
- Lore Fragments that were in the Exploration category have been moved to the Lore category

### Destiny Board Crafting Bonuses

**Leather Armor**

With the addition of the new Dragonslayer Armor items in the leather category, Focus Cost Efficiency and Quality bonuses have been adjusted for all Leather Armor items in order to keep the total available crafting bonuses equal across all armor types.
- Focus Cost Efficiency to all Leather Armors/Heads/Shoes per level:
  - 15 → 12.5

- Quality Bonus to all Leather Armors/Heads/Shoes per level:
  - 0.375 → 0.3125

**Potions**

With the addition of the new Lifeward Potion in this update, we have also looked at Focus Cost Efficiency bonuses for potions. Since certain potions require expensive artifact ingredients, we have decided to weight Focus Cost Efficiency bonuses away from these and towards those which do not require more specialized ingredients. In doing so, the total alchemy bonuses will be more fairly distributed between base potions and more specialized potions.

We chose this specific approach as it benefits the majority of crafters, and keeps alchemy competitive with cooking as the majority of the bonus comes from simple ingredients. This also now more closely mirrors the Focus Cost distribution for weapons and armor, where base items provide more of the overall bonus due to their accessible reagent costs.

The Lifeward Potion is grouped with those which provide less Focus Cost Efficiency bonuses.
- Heal Brewer, Energy Brewer, Gigantify Brewer, Resistance Brewer, Sticky Brewer, Poison Brewer, Invisibility Brewer, and Bootlegger
  - Focus Cost Efficiency group bonus per level: 18 → 22.5

- Calming Brewer, Cleansing Brewer, Acid Brewer, Berserk Brewer, Hellfire Brewer, Gathering Brewer, Tornado Brewer, and Lifeward Brewer:
  - Focus Cost Efficiency group bonus per level: 18 → 11.25

### Destiny Board Improvements

To improve visual clarity, specialization nodes on the Destiny Board with the same parent node have been combined into one. This does not change the behavior of specializations in the game, but visually simplifies the Destiny Board.
- To keep a sense of accomplishment, individual specializations are represented by badges encircling the combined specialization node
  - Individual specializations can be previewed by hovering over the combined specialization node
  - Each badge visually reflects its specialization level

- Opening a specialization node shows a list of all specializations inside
  - Tracking in the HUD Tracker and activating Quick Learn for the specializations can be toggled for both individual specializations or all at once

- Specialization details and recommended builds have been moved to the new Details tab within the Respec UI in the combined specialization node

### Armory Improvements

The Armory's Metrics section now includes a second page with a detailed breakdown of a build’s performance, making it easier to evaluate and compare them.
- This view displays average per-hour statistics, including damage dealt, healing done, kills, deaths, and other combat metrics, alongside each build's Average Item Power
- Players who want to compare builds at a glance can also enable a new option to display Average Item Power directly in the Armory's equipment list

### Other Changes

- Resource Guardians now spawn in Tier 6+ regions in the Outlands, instead of around resource hotspots
  - A Guardian only spawns in a biome in which its resource is present
  - Resource Guardians spawn without any preannouncement and are neither shown on the Region or the World Map

- Furniture on Player and Guild Islands can now be placed directly next to each other
  - If you become trapped, remember that you can use Journey Back for free while mounted
  - If you're unmounted, you can use your mount-slot ability to teleport out of the island, similar to leaving a Randomized Dungeon
    - This also works while overweight

- Focus Fire Protection for mobs has been reworked to scale more smoothly if a large numbers of players attack the same target
  - This allows powerful mobs, including Dragon Attacks, to remain challenging for large groups without disproportionately increasing their strength against smaller groups

- The aggression of Resource and Hide mobs has been reduced
  - More creatures are now passive, and aggressive mobs are less likely to engage players from a distance

- After being knocked down for more than 30 seconds in Blue and Yellow Zone open-world regions, you can now choose to respawn at the nearest region entrance
  - This option is unavailable for players with Outlaw status, in Faction Warfare combat, or while inside an open-PvP zone

- The target selection circle is now red, making it easier to distinguish selected targets from enemy spell effect areas
- Animal growth times have been standardized across player islands and the Outlands
-  Ground-targeted and auto-targeted spells can now automatically select the previewed target when no target is currently selected

### Combat Balance Changes

**Mob Debuff Changes**

> _After observing both elite and standard groups in PvE, the performance gap between groups that consistently reach the maximum debuff caps and those that only apply part of those debuffs has become too wide. The debuff caps applied to players and PvE enemies have therefore been decoupled.
Adjusting these caps provides a healthier baseline for balancing PvE encounters, without excessively punishing standard groups or allowing highly optimized groups to trivialize encounters. Paired with additional changes to specific spells, this should also give groups more flexibility in how they reach the new caps._

- Maximum Health decrease vs. mobs: 50% → 25%
- Maximum damage decrease vs. mobs: 80% → 50%

**Axes**

> _Axes have become much stronger in small-scale combat following the recent buff to Adrenaline Boost. Its behavior has now been refined to give opponents more counterplay._

- Adrenaline Boost (all Axes)
  - Now expires if the caster does not directly damage an enemy every 3s

**Fire Staffs**

> _Flame Blast has been too powerful as a ganking tool. Its movement speed buff for allies has therefore been removed, while retaining the mobility granted when self-casting._

- Flame Blast (all Fire Staffs)
  - No longer grants move speed to other allies

**Frost Staffs**

> _Avalanche has received increased damage, while its knockback against mobs has been adjusted to improve its performance in solo PvE._

- Avalanche (Hoarfrost Staff)
  - Snowball rolling damage: 120 → 160
  - Knockback vs. mobs: 10m → 5m
  - Max. air time vs. mobs: 0.8 → 1.25

> _Frost Lance has received its missing AoE Escalation, along with several other spells listed below. Its damage has also been reduced to limit its effectiveness as a very long-range poke tool with limited counterplay._

- Frost Lance (all Frost Staffs)
  - Damage: 148 → 134

> _Following its recent buffs, Freezing Wind has become a top performer in solo PvP. While the instant effect has proven more effective than the previous version, its root duration has been reduced and its hitbox has been significantly adjusted to give opponents more room for counterplay._

- Freezing Wind (Frost Staff)
  - Hitbox area now grows over 0.4s
  - Total hitbox duration: 2s → 0.6s
  - Root duration: 2.5s → 2s

**Maces**

> _Blessed Aurora’s healing on auto-attacks has been increased to encourage a new Oathkeepers playstyle in smaller-scale PvP, focusing less on the shield’s immediate effects and more on maximizing auto-attack uptime._

- Blessed Aurora (Oathkeepers)
  - Healing on auto-attack: 30 → 66

> _Gravitas has remained unpopular for some time. With more weapons and builds in modern Albion relying on blinks and dashes, the Morning Star’s root has become less effective. While the weapon remains a candidate for a larger rework in the future, its cooldowns have now been improved to help it better keep pace with competing options._

- Gravitas (Morning Star)
  - Cooldown: 30s → 20s
  - Recast cooldown: 40s → 30s
  - Cooldown scaling per 100 IP: -1s → -0.5s

> _Following the reduction to the maximum damage decrease that can be applied to mobs, single-source, damage-decreasing abilities have also been adjusted to better fit the new cap._

- Shrinking Curse (Incubus Mace)
  - Damage decrease debuff vs. mobs: 40% → 25%

**Nature Staffs**

> _Rampant Staff has received targeting and indicator adjustments to make Spirit Animal easier to use. Its hitbox now begins slightly behind the cast position, while its cast range has been increased to offset the resulting loss in maximum forward reach._

- Spirit Animal (Rampant Staff)
  - Hitbox now starts 3m behind the cast position
  - Cast range: 12m → 15m

> _Druidic Staff has fallen out of the group PvP meta. To give it more power and flexibility, Spiritual Seed can now be recast a third time, at the cost of an additional cooldown penalty._

- Spiritual Seed (Druidic Staff)
  - Can now be recast a third time
  - Cooldown per recast:
    - 1st: 20s
    - 2nd: 20s
    - 3rd: 30s

  - Stand time on cast: 0.4s → 0.25s

**Quarterstaffs**

> _Following the reduction to the maximum damage decrease that can be applied to mobs, single-source, damage-decreasing abilities have also been adjusted to better fit the new cap._

- Fatal Blade (Black Monk Staff)
  - Damage decrease debuff per tick vs. mobs: 10% → 8% (total: 30% → 24%)

- Forceful Swing (all Quarterstaffs)
  - Damage decrease debuff vs. mobs: 50% → 20%

**Shapeshifter Staffs**

> _The Panther transformation’s auto-attack passive has received increased damage to encourage and better reward fighting while transformed._

To address this, Sinister Swipes’ cooldown with Shapeshifter Charges has been further increased._

> _For the upcoming season, all Resilience Penetration has been set to zero as a seasonal test. The goal is to shift the meta more strongly away from Plate melee DPS and create space for players to rediscover and experiment with different compositions.

However, modern Albion offers significantly more mobility across weapon lines, while player positioning and engagement strategies have also evolved.
This season will therefore serve as a test of how ZvZ combat develops without Resilience Penetration. The results will be closely observed, and further adjustments may follow based on how the meta develops._
