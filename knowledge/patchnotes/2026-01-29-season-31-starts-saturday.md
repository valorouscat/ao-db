### Season Changes

This season brings changes to the way **Stored Energy **is paid out. Rather than happening in full on the final Invasion Day of the season, it is now also paid out on the **mid-season Invasion Day**. The first guild to conquer a territory after Invasion Day begins will be the recipients of this batch of Season Points.

In addition, changes have been made to **Might **requirements across a number of challenges: Hellgates, Corrupted Dungeons, the Depths, Hideout Power Cores, Smugglers, and Castles and Castle Outposts. The 2v2 and 5v5 Hellgate categories have also been merged into a single **Hellgates challenge**.
