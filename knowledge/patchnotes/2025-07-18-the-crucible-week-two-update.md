# The Crucible: Week Two Update

_date: 2025-07-18 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1805431065368641_

**The Crucible **is now halfway through, and a fierce battle is unfolding at the top of the leaderboard. **Zizaran** has narrowly overtaken **Zerator** after a week of relentless grinding, with both creators trading blows as their guilds fight to stay ahead. **Diables & ZWIERZAAK**’s team has surged into third, while former frontrunner **Quin69** now sits in sixth. **Metashi** is holding on in the top 10, with **Knekro** just outside it. But determined challengers like **REVENANT**, **Nozadah**, **ZeusGhostz**, **CAHOS_GAMING**, and **NorgannonHH**, are making sure every spot is hard-won.

The first half of the contest has made one thing clear: these first-time Albion players are diving headfirst into every corner of the game. From leading warbands in the Outlands to grinding the Depths for hours on end, they’re making the most of every opportunity to climb the ranks.

And don’t forget: this weekend is your last chance to guarantee the exclusive **Brown Alpaca** mount skin! Until **Sunday, July 20** at **09:30 UTC**, simply gift 2 subs on Twitch to any **Crucible participant**, **AOCP Creator**, or [**AlbionTV**](https://www.twitch.tv/albiononline) to claim this fluffy companion instantly. Just look for the Alpaca icon on top of the chat during their **Albion livestreams** to know you’re in the right place!

The Crucible runs until **August 2**. Keep an eye on the [**leaderboard**](https://albiononline.com/crucible/) and your favorite creators’ streams as the contest barrels toward an epic finish.
