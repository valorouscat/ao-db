# Season 27 Is on the Way!

_date: 2025-03-21 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1794830910900254_

As **Season 26** enters its final stretch, the next battle for supremacy is already in sight! The **shorter season format** has kept the action fast and relentless, and **Season 27** promises another intense two-month race to the top.

Are you ready for battle? **Season 27 begins on April 19**, with **Albion Asia** starting at **00:00 UTC**, followed by **Albion Americas **and **Albion Europe** at **11:00 UTC**.

### Season Rewards

Players can once again participate in the **Conqueror’s Challenge** to claim exclusive **Avatar Sets**, **Fame buffs**, and the coveted **Artifacts** needed to forge the next batch of powerful **Crystal Weapons** – set to be unveiled after the season’s conclusion. Earn **Season Points** through various activities and by amassing **Might** alongside your fellow guild members to get these rewards!

### Season Schedule Highlights

- **April 19**: Season start and Invasion Day
- **May 17**: Mid-Season Invasion Day, double scoring begins
- **June 14**: Final Invasion Day
- **June 16**: Season ends

> _Note: There's been a slight change to the schedule, and this season will have **12-day breaks** before and after, instead of the shorter off-season that was initially planned._

For the full season schedule, see [**this forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/209673-Season-27-Schedules/).

### New Conqueror’s Challenge History Tab

A new **Conqueror’s Challenge History **tab is on the way. This new UI will make it easier to overview rewards and revisit past seasons. Moreover, Conqueror’s Chest rewards will now appear as a list instead of a single chest, letting you claim items individually or all at once for better inventory management.

### New to Guild Seasons? Here's What You Need to Know!

**Guild Seasons** are a key part of Albion's competitive landscape, where guilds battle over two months to claim territories, earn Season Points, rise up the rankings and earn coveted rewards. Participating in a Guild Season allows you to contribute to your guild's success through various activities – even if you’re not focused on PvP, you can still help your guild gather **Season Points** and unlock valuable rewards!
