# Dev Talk: Realm Divided Introduction

_date: 2025-10-15 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813674388900430_

A new Dev Talk is here! In this video, **Game Director Moritz Bokelmann** takes a first look at Albion’s next major update, **Realm Divided**, scheduled for **November 24**. This update focuses on revamping the game’s **Faction Warfare** systems, making it an active, strategic, and ever-evolving source of Royal Continent PvP.

### Goals

Faction Warfare has long been a core element of gameplay on the Royal Continent, and offers many players their first taste of open-world combat and group PvP. However, in some ways it’s struggled to fulfil its potential, with fights too scattered and infrequent, and travel times too long.

The next update aims to improve the Faction Warfare experience in several key ways:

- Make it an **active, ongoing conflict**
- Tie together Albion’s **economy, travel, strategy, and combat**
- Bridge casual and **large-scale PvP**
- Allow **players of all levels** to have an impact

### Provinces

Realm Divided reworks Faction Warfare’s territorial mechanics into a new system: **Provinces**.

- Provinces are made up of **several different regions** on the Royal Continent
- These create clear **frontiers** between factions, reorganizing the old frontline system
- A system of rotating territorial wars leads to **connected, meaningful battles**
- Players are drawn into **large-scale conflict**, with consequences for the wider campaign
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/6b63436d4ac806fe0582cb2f5f9eade8ac7a5193.jpeg)

### Faction Fortresses

Within each Province stands a **Faction Fortress**, which provides control over the area.

- These bring **Fortification** gameplay to the Royal Continent
- Players of all levels can now participate in **large-scale sieges**
- Capturing or defending a Fortress can **push the frontier forward**, or help repel the enemy
- Each Fortress fight carries weight, influencing the **momentum of the broader war**
