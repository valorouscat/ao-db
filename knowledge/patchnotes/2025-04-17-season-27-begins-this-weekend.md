# Season 27 Begins This Weekend!

_date: 2025-04-17 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1797185861634401_

A new **Guild Season** starts this weekend, and after the backstabs, steamrolls, and razor-thin finishes that defined **Season 26**, the stage is set for another wild ride.

Guilds across Albion are once again rallying their forces for a season-long war that will test their strategy, strength, and resolve in a battle for lasting glory. Tune in to [**twitch.tv/albiononline**](http://twitch.tv/albiononline) to witness history in the making as the **AlbionTV** crew brings you live coverage of the opening **Invasion Day**, complete with commentary and analysis:

- **Albion Asia**: April 19, 14:00 UTC
- **Albion Europe**: April 19, 18:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas**: April 20, 01:00 UTC

### What to Expect This Season?

The **Might requirements** for Treasures, Crystal Creatures, Smugglers, and Gathering categories have all been reduced. For the first three categories, Season Point payouts were also adjusted to maintain balance in total output. Gathering, however, retains its full reward potential – giving smaller or economy-focused guilds greater opportunity to work up their way up the rankings.

For more info, check out our [**Season 27 preview**](https://albiononline.com/news/season-27-coming), and for the full schedule see this [**forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/209673-Season-27-Schedules/).
