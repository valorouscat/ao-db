### How Does it Work?

In the Royal Hunt, creators form **guilds** to complete **missions** that cover a wide range of content, from gathering resources to slaying Dragons. Using a dedicated **Guild Journal**, guilds earn points by completing these missions, and by doing so progress through a series of **ranks**. By reaching each rank, streamers can unlock **giveaways** for their communities, including **Dragon Coins**, minted from **real silver**, for those who attain the highest rank:

- **Rank I: **Premium Codes
- **Rank II:** Learning Points Packs
- **Rank III:** Gold Packs
- **Rank IV:** Mount Skins
- **Rank V:** Vanity Armors
- **Rank VI:** Gifted subs, and Essentials Packs containing Premium, Gold, Learning Points, and Scrolls of Fame
- **Rank VII:** One Dragon Coin, and Essentials Packs containing Premium, Gold, Learning Points, and Scrolls of Fame
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/a508c498ec9ccd14e0140846c8a9033b7e6d7995.jpeg)

The larger a streamer’s audience, the more points they must accumulate to reach each rank, but the greater the amount of rewards they have to give away!

The **top 10**, meanwhile, will receive **additional Dragon Coins**, based on their final position, to raffle amongst their members:

- **1st - 3rd place**: 3 additional Dragon Coins
- **4th - 5th place**: 2 additional Dragon Coins
- **6th - 10th place**: 1 additional Dragon Coin
To see a creator’s channel, simply click their name on the [**leaderboard**](https://albiononline.com/royal-hunt/). Follow your favorites now to support their dragon hunting and have the chance to earn these giveaways!
