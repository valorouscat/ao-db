# Dev Talk: Development Update October 2026

_date: 2026-10-16 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846651706680832_

It's been over a month since **Dragonfire** launched, and our latest **Dev Talk** looks back at the update and ahead to what's next for Albion. Co-Creative Director **Michael Schwahn** discusses player feedback and recent adjustments, while Game Director **Moritz Bokelmann** reveals plans for the return of the 1v1 Arena and a major overhaul of Albion's seasons and challenges coming early next year. Take a look:

### Looking Back at Dragonfire

Since **Dragonfire** launched, players have been exploring the **Ancient Lands**, battling Dragons, and taking on the **Dragon Sanctuary**, where **Regaro** and his party achieved the **world's first **kill of Azaryon, the Fire Dragon. Congratulations to them!

Alongside these achievements, the team has been listening closely to **player feedback**. One key takeaway is that players want to visit the **Ancient Lands** more frequently, which has already led to several adjustments:

- **Small Portals in Yellow Zones** are now **permanently available**, offering more frequent access to the Ancient Lands with slightly reduced rewards
  - **Small Portals in Lethal Zones** remain **unchanged**, preserving their high-risk, high-reward gameplay

- A new** Item Freeze** mechanic protects items brought into these areas
  - Only loot acquired inside can be lost

Beyond the Ancient Lands, the team has also been making adjustments to other Dragonfire features:

- The **Dragon Sanctuary** continues to receive adjustments to its difficulty and rewards:
  - **Difficulty** is still being evaluated to keep the dungeon challenging but achievable
  - **Drake Egg **drop rates in higher-tier dungeons are another focus, aiming to better reward groups that take on tougher challenges

- **Open-World Dragons** in the Outlands now grant significantly **more loot and Might**

### What's Next for Albion?

Dragonfire was one of Albion's largest development projects in recent years, and the team has only recently been able to shift its focus to new projects. However, there are already some exciting plans on the horizon.

**The 1v1 Arena Returns**

Following positive feedback from its earlier run, the **1v1 Arena will return**:

- It will be available for the duration of the** next Guild Season**
- Players can once again test builds and compete in one-on-one PvP

**A New Direction for Seasons**

Looking further ahead, the next major content update, planned for early next year, will introduce a substantial overhaul of **seasons and challenges**.

- Currently, **Guild Seasons** primarily matter to players involved in competitive guild gameplay
- Other progression systems – Adventurer’s Challenge, Conqueror’s Challenge, Faction Warfare campaign – operate alongside them with different rules, timelines, and rewards
- This makes it difficult for players to understand what's happening in Albion each season and how they can take part

The goal is to bring these systems into a clearer, more **unified seasonal framework**:

- **Guild competition** will remain important, but **won't be a requirement** for participation
- **Existing challenges** will be integrated into a more **cohesive seasonal progression** system, making it easier to understand what's happening and track progress
- **Meaningful objectives** should be available throughout the season for players who enjoy different activities across Albion

But the overhaul goes beyond reorganizing existing systems. The team also wants to make individual seasons **more engaging**, taking inspiration from the Keeper Uprising:

- Future seasons could introduce new activities, world changes, or special challenges, giving each season its **own identity**
- **Not every season** will feature a major event, but the aim is to give players new things to discover and work toward

**More Meaningful Rewards**

**Seasonal rewards **are another important part of the overhaul. Since players have different goals, the team is exploring ways to make **progression more flexible**:

- Players could have more choice over **which rewards they pursue first**, rather than following a strictly linear progression path
- The goal is to make **progression feel worthwhile** throughout the entire season, with **meaningful rewards** for different types of players
Ultimately, these changes aim to make seasons more inclusive, engaging, and rewarding for everyone playing Albion.

We're hard at work on this overhaul, along with other new features and content planned for Albion. There's lots more to share, so stay tuned for future Dev Talks. In the meantime, head over to the video and let us know what you think in the comments!
