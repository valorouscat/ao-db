# Dev Talk: The Ancient Lands

_date: 2026-08-13 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1840944183776981_

The [**Dragonfire**](https://albiononline.com/update/dragonfire) update, launching **August 31**, expands Albion with new places to explore, powerful enemies to fight, exciting rewards to earn, and, most importantly, Dragons. One of the update’s biggest new features is the **Ancient Lands**: a completely new region combining exploration, PvE, and PvP with meaningful decision-making.

In this **Dev Talk**, Co-Creative Director **Michael Schwahn**, Game Designer **Marco Nogielsky**, and Environment Artist **Johannes Geier** present the mechanics, visual design, and atmosphere of this brand-new area.

### Entering the Ancient Lands

**Portals** to the Ancient Lands appear throughout the **open world**, with different portal sizes accommodating different **party sizes**.

- **Small portals** are intended for **solo** players and **small groups**
  - These portals appear with very **little warning**, allowing spontaneous expeditions

- **Large portals** support parties of up to **20 players**
  - These portals are** announced in advance**, providing organized PvP content for larger groups

- Within each portal size, parties are **matched** with groups of similar sizes
  - A solo player, for example, will not suddenly face a full five-player party

The **type of PvP** inside depends on where the portal appears:

- Portals in the **Outlands** and **Red Zones** lead to a **lethal PvP** version of the Ancient Lands
- Portals in **Yellow Zones** lead to a **non-lethal Orange Zone** version

### Traversing the Islands

Once inside, each party begins on its **own floating island** at the outer edge of the region, initially separated from all other groups. To progress through the Ancient Lands, players must first defeat Keeper mobs to complete Fame objectives.

- Completing the island quest causes a **Keystone** to appear
- The Keystone can be used to **activate** one of the island’s teleporters, opening a route to a neighboring island
  - Activated teleporters can be used by every group
  - **Keystones can be moved** between teleporters to redirect the available routes

The **Minimap** can be used to distinguish between inactive, newly activated, and established connections

- Each new route expands the possibilities for travel while making **rival groups** harder to track and encounters more likely
- This shifting network makes** map awareness** and control over movement an important part of every expedition
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/d34578c749c65fa2c9c580497b91b61ab12a91c3.jpeg)

Players can **extract their loot** and **leave** through any entrance portal.

- The party returns to their original entry point in the open world, temporarily protected and invisible

### Drake Objectives

**Four Drake islands **are marked on the Minimap. These contain some of the best **rewards** available in the Ancient Lands, including:

- Treasure Chests
- Dragon Blood
- Spirit Waystone Fragments
  - These help unlock access to the **Dragon Sanctuary**

****

Once all four Drakes have been defeated, the **Fire Drake** appears at the center of the region:

- The Fire Drake protects the Ancient Lands’ **largest reward**
- Defeating it can provide **complete Spirit Waystones**
Parties are incentivized to proactively repel rivals, dominate key territory, and seize objectives before competitors can intervene.

### Bleedout

Inevitably, group battles occur that can leave players behind even when their party ultimately wins. To give groups an opportunity to recover, a new **Bleedout **knockdown state has been introduced in the Ancient Lands.

- This exists in both **Orange Zone **and **Black Zone **variants
- Defeated players **weaken over time**, but can hold out longer while their party remains nearby
- Allies can **revive** them, although revived players receive a **debuff** that lasts for the rest of their time in the region
This gives groups a chance to recover after contested fights, but repeated losses still carry a lasting cost.

### Art and Environment

The Ancient Lands are a hidden, magic-saturated realm of shattered floating islands, once home to the **ancient Keepers** and** Dragons**.

- Streams of** concentrated magic** flow through the air, while megalithic gates, ritual statues, and ancient channeling structures reveal the remains of a powerful Keeper civilization
- At the center stands a **grand temple** built both to honor and control the Dragons, which the Keepers eventually put to sleep through powerful rituals
- The environment balances **natural beauty** with **constant danger**, from flower-covered meadows to unforgiving wilderness
- **Distinct areas** include swamps with lakes and waterfalls, woodlands dominated by enormous trees, and landscapes filled with the remains of giant creatures
The **Ancient Lands** have been designed as a unique addition to Albion, from the way players explore the region and interact with its mechanics to its creatures, visual identity, and atmosphere.
