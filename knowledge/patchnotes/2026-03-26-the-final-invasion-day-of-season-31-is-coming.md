# The Final Invasion Day of Season 31 is Coming

_date: 2026-03-26 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1827626365770134_

**Season 31** is entering its final stretch. The top ranks are beginning to solidify, but not without tension beneath the surface. While some guilds are on the brink of securing their place in Albion’s history, others are regrouping, forming new coalitions, and already setting the stage for what comes next.

As the **final Invasion Day** closes in, here’s how the balance of power stands:

- **Albion Europe**: As the final Invasion Day of Season 31 approaches, _Skoggangr_ are firmly in control and set to secure their first victory. Coalition members _G2U _hold second place, with _Squirtle Squad_ looking solid in third unless an unlikely last-minute upset changes the standings. This follows a brutal mid-season war that defined the season on Europe. _STORM_ entered the season aiming for a third consecutive win, but after sustained fighting across the inner ring, _Skoggangr_ and their allies took control and maintained their lead through the final stretch.
	The fighting may be calming down, but tensions are far from over. _Woof Woof_ has already declared their intention to push for a victory in Season 32, and internal instability has begun to show within _Skoggangr_’s ranks. Last week, a high-profile player left the guild after taking a large amount of resources with them, including over 700 million Silver and 10,000 Siphoned Energy, before joining the newly formed guild “_Pyramid Scheme_.” We’ll break down the full story of this season’s war in the coming weeks. For now, though the battlefield may be quieter, the next conflict is already taking shape.
- **Albion Americas**: Following their return and successful campaign to reclaim control of the Outlands, _IFures _are set to secure their fourth season victory, becoming only the second guild in Albion Online’s history to reach this milestone.
	With first place effectively decided, attention has shifted to the battle for second. _Unslave _currently hold that position, but they are under increasing pressure from _Make Albion Great Again_, who have recently launched a series of raids and territory attacks to close the gap. With around 120,000 Season Points separating them, a late push could still change the outcome. The battle for third place remains even more open, with several guilds still in contention. While first place is secured, the rest of the top positions are still up for grabs!
- **Albion Asia**: After an early-season clash between _Maharlika_ and _Summers_ over first place, _Summers_ have taken control and now look set to secure their second victory, denying _Maharlika_ the top spot this time around. _Maharlika_ have since regrouped under the _WPZ Alliance_ tag, but their focus has shifted to stabilizing rather than reclaiming first place. Meanwhile, _AFTERMATH_ are firmly holding second place. Operating without a larger alliance structure, they’ve secured their position through consistent performance across the season.
	While the current rankings look settled, new conflicts are already forming. The return of _The Flying Dutchman_, combined with a coalition between the _DB_, _MMT_, and _CRIT _alliances, signals a potential challenge to the current dominant force on the server, led by _Fuckcall_, and their control over the Outlands. How this plays out, well, we'll see on the final Invasion Day this Saturday…
