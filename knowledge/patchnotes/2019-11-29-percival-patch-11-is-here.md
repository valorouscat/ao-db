# Percival Patch 11 is Here

_date: 2019-11-29 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/2551782186262400832_

The [**Avalonian Invasion**](https://albiononline.com/en/news/outlands-report-1-threats-and-opportunities) is in full swing, guilds are battling it out in a unique standalone season... and **Percival Patch 11** is here! Today's patch brings several important updates to combat in Albion, in particular:

- The [**zerg debuff**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/119764-Disarray-Zerg-Debuff/?postID=964642#post964642) values have been updated to more effectively reward smaller armies
- Zerg debuffs no longer apply **during GvGs**, regardless of how many allied players are in the territory
- **Healing Sickness** duration has been increased 
- **Siege Ballista** damage has been increased
- Numerous other melee, ranged, and magic skills have been updated 

Michael Schwahn, Combat Designer for Albion Online, describes the intent behind these changes: "We wanted to shake up the healer meta a bit, and to push the siege ballista as a viable counter to other battle mounts. We also felt it was time to reduce the utility of Parry Strike, in order to open up more of a variety with bruiser builds." These changes will play out over the remainder of the standalone season, which **[runs until January 20](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/119885-Standalone-Event-Season-Schedule/)**.

## Additional Changes

In a first for the world of Albion, **Uncle Frost** now appears as a boss in certain rare dungeons! Group and solo **Keeper Randomized Dungeons** in all zones have a chance to end in a special **Yuletide Dungeon**, where you'll face off against this mysterious foe. Beat him to unlock Christmas-themed loot, including a chance at holiday-themed **mount skins**.

Also with today's patch, a new **Adventurer Avatar** is available at the Vanity Merchant. This new item joins the **Adventurer Avatar Ring** in a growing list of vanity items – trade in unclaimed Adventurer's Challenge Avatar Rings for Adventurer Tokens to unlock these new items!

For a complete list of changes, please see the [**official changelog**](https://albiononline.com/en/changelog/PERCIVAL%20PATCH%2011).
