# Season 32 Starts Saturday

_date: 2026-04-15 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1830163047254383_

The war for the Outlands continues on** Saturday, April 18**, as [**Season 32**](https://albiononline.com/news/season-32-coming) begins. Guilds across all servers are preparing for the next chapter, and this time, we’ve prepared something special for you!

Start times by server:

- **Albion Asia**: April 18, 00:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas **and **Albion Europe**: April 18, 11:00 UTC
Season highlights:

- **April 18: **Season start and Invasion Day
- **May 16: **Mid-season Invasion Day, double scoring begins, storage payout
- **June 13:** Final Invasion Day, storage payout
- **June 15: **Season ends
For full season changes and schedules, see this [**forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/221258-Season-32-Schedules/).
