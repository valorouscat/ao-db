# Season 34 Starts Soon

_date: 2026-09-03 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842846814442809_

In a mysterious realm far beyond the clouds, centuries-old dragons have awakened. And they don’t seem too happy about it honestly, leaving a trail of scorched earth and scorched adventurers in their wake. But for Albion’s guilds, this means new challenges and opportunities when **Season 34** kicks off on **September 12**!

Start times:

- **Albion Asia**: September 12, 00:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas** and **Europe**: September 12, 11:00 UTC

### Season Changes

With brave souls exploring aerial islands and encountering ancient beasts, two new Guild Might Levels have been added this season: **Ancient Lands **and **Dragons**.

On the battlefield, meanwhile, **Resilience Penetration** is being **removed** for this season. Since combat in Albion has evolved a lot since this mechanic was introduced, removing it for Season 34 will allow us to see how large-scale PvP plays out in its absence. The effects of this change will be monitored throughout the season, and adjustments made if necessary – for a full list of weapons and spells affected by this change, see the [**Dragonfire patchnotes**](https://albiononline.com/changelog/dragonfire-update).

### Rewards

This season’s rewards include the brand-new **Flameward Avatar** in Silver and Crystal variants, allowing you to flaunt your dragonslaying prowess.

On top of that you can secure valuable loot **chests**, **60-day Fame boosts**, and **legacy avatars**. You can already start progressing in the Conqueror’s Challenge, so get out there now and earn some Might!

### Key Dates

- **September 12: **Season start and Invasion Day
- **October 10: **Mid-season Invasion Day, double scoring begins, storage payout
- **November 7: **Final Invasion Day, storage payout
- **November 9: **Season ends
For full season schedules, see [**this forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/224318-Season-34-Schedules/).

### New to Guild Seasons? Here's What You Need to Know!

**Guild Seasons** are a key part of Albion's competitive landscape, where guilds battle over two months to claim territories, earn Season Points, rise up the rankings and earn coveted rewards. Participating in a Guild Season allows you to contribute to your guild's success through various activities. Even if you’re not focused on PvP, you can still help your guild gather **Season Points** and unlock valuable rewards!
