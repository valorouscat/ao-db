# Dev Talk: Keeper Uprising

_date: 2026-06-25 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165549441_

In this** Dev Talk**, Co-Creative Director **Michael Schwahn** introduces the **Keeper Uprising**, a new event that begins on **July 4** and runs throughout the entire season. Featuring evolving encounters, event-exclusive rewards, and Lore Fragments, the Keeper Uprising unfolds a new story over the course of two months. Watch the latest Dev Talk below:

### An Unfolding Story

The Keeper Uprising will **develop progressively** throughout **Guild Season 33**, culminating in Albion’s next major update.

During the event, players will encounter a splinter group within the Keepers known as the **Ashborn**. As the uprising spreads, the Ashborn presence will grow across Albion, creating unusual encounters throughout the world:

- **New Ashborn enemies** will appear across Albion, featuring new visuals, abilities, and challenges
- Keeper enemies will begin appearing in** unfamiliar places** where players would not normally expect to find them
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/749705bc975814f1cbc8a2473199498a669e7dd5.jpeg)

The Keeper Uprising encourages **exploration**, inviting adventurers to discover the hidden depths and secrets of the world.

### Event Missions and Rewards

The Keeper Uprising introduces a series of special **missions** themed around the Keeper faction.

[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/e8909b78ffc84c3ddc90c465de0ecc93ccb4ac99.jpeg)
- These missions are **only available during the event**
- They work similarly to Albion Journal missions and span a **wide variety of activities **across Albion
- Each mission features its **own objective and reward**
[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/00ef51e7ce9bb1568b751d4c792e4826c085eb13.jpeg)

As players complete these missions, they will gradually unlock **exclusive vanity rewards** – the **Ashborn Armor**, **Helmet**, and **Avatar Ring**.

### Discovering Albion’s Lore

This event also introduces a brand-new feature for explorers and lore enthusiasts: **Lore Fragments**. The first collection, known as **Keeper Memories**, provides insight into the Keepers' motivations, struggles, and view of the world.

[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/269d5830737c7fb91224991a69c81d67f68a2096.jpeg)
- Keeper Memories can be discovered in two ways:
  - Found in **specific locations** throughout Albion, including secret passages and concealed areas
    - Several **new hidden locations** have been added to the world, meaning familiar places may now contain secrets waiting to be discovered

  - Obtained as **drops** from certain mobs
    - These drops are **untradable** and cannot be purchased from the Marketplace

- Keeper Memories are **recorded** **permanently **in the **Albion Journal** under the Exploration category, even after the event has ended
  - This lets you revisit discovered memories and access **hints** that point you toward those still missing

[](//assets.albiononline.com/uploads/media/default/media/20941a40de86ff39f6abe3bd27788db7b2fa5079.jpeg)

Additionally, several Keeper Uprising missions require you to collect a number of Keeper Memories, helping you progress towards the event rewards.

### Guild Season Integration

The Keeper Uprising also ties directly into the upcoming Guild Season:

- The event has a dedicated **Guild Challenge** that rewards a substantial amount of Season Points
- Defeating event-related enemies grants **additional Might progress**, giving guilds another reason to take part
The world you know is under threat! Join the Keeper Uprising on **July 4** and venture across Albion as new encounters emerge and the mystery behind the Ashborn begins to unfold. Stay tuned for more info as the event approaches…
