# Season 33 Starts July 4

_date: 2026-06-19 · source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1835871199306633_

Rumors are spreading throughout the lands of an [**uprising among the Keepers of Albion**](https://albiononline.com/news/keeper-uprising-coming), sowing doubt and distraction among even its most seasoned warriors. But the ongoing war for dominance of the Outlands continues, as guilds prepare for battle in these most uncertain times…

**Season 33 **begins **July 4**, and Albion will never be the same again.

Start times:

- **Albion Asia**: July 4, 00:00 UTC
- **Albion Americas **and **Europe**: July 4, 11:00 UTC

### Season Changes

This season introduces a one-off **Keeper Uprising** category to the **Conqueror’s Challenge**, replacing the Crystal Creatures category for the duration of the season. More details about this will be revealed as further information is gathered about this brewing turmoil among the Keepers.

In addition, some changes have been made to territory **Season Point **generation and **control costs**, along with adjustments to **energy destruction **from territory raids and **Hideout **durability. For a full schedule and a detailed rundown of these changes, see [**this forum post**](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/223209-Season-33-Schedules/).

### Season Schedule Highlights

- **July 4: **Season start and Invasion Day
- **August 1: **Mid-season Invasion Day, double scoring begins, storage payout
- **August 29:** Final Invasion Day, storage payout
- **August 31: **Season ends
